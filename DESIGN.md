
1. BASIC INFO
Title: Falling Socks
Tagline: Socks to be you!
Genre: Endless-falling, roguelite
Platform: Mobile
Target Audience: People with fingers

2.DESCRIPTION

Falling socks is a one-finger arcade roguelite about a brave little sock diving into the mysterious world beneath the washing machine to find its missing partner. 
Presented from a fixed side perspective in a colorful, stylised 2D world, the game combines simple arcade left and right controls with a risk and reward system: the more objects you collect, the heavier you become, the faster you fall and the harder it is to steer.
Players must dodge obstacles, gather lost treasures and decide how far they are willing to fall before cashing out their rewards. 
Each run provides resources to improve their sock and venture deeper into the increasingly strange world below. 

3. SETTING

When one sock misteriously disappears during laundry day its maching partner refuses to accept a life of loneliness and dives headfirst into the unknown. 
What begins as a journey from a pile of dirty laundry quickly turns into an adventure through spinning washing machines, rusty pipes, underground sewers and places no pieces of clothing was never meant to see. 
Along the way, our little hero discovers a world of all the things humanity has ever lost in the wash: buttons, coins, jewellery and countless forgotten treasures.
Somewhere, at the very bottom, lies the legendary pitt where all missing things go to fade away. Hopefully, its partner is still there. 

4. MAIN GAME MECHANICS

Falling: The sock falls automatically through vertical scrolling enviroments. Players only control its horizontal movement by swiping left or right with one finger. The objective is to dodge obstacles. collect valuables and survive for as long as possible.
Weight: Players encounter buttons, coins and other lost valuables. These collectibles are the game currency but they come at a cost. The richer you become, the harder the game gets. Every object adds weight to the sock, increasing its falling speed but reducing its manoeuvrability. 
Damage: The sock's fabric acts as its health. Colliding with obstacles damages the fabric and repeated impacts tear the fabric appart, ending the run. 
Risk and Reward: At certain points during the run, players reach safe areas where they can choose to bank their cllected rewards or continue falling deeper. Banking secures the rewards but ends the run while pushing forwards offers greater rewards at the risk of losing everything. 
Progression: Between runs the currency can be invested in permanent upgrades. Stronger fabric increases durability, improved stearing makes high-speed falls easier to control. Players can also unlock cosmetic customization, including new sock colours, patterns, patches and designs. 
Biomes: The descent takes players into a series of bizarre enviroments, starting beneath the laundry basket, continuing through the washing machine, descending into a maze of pipesand sewers and eventually reaching more abstract biomes like the pitt where things get lost forever. Each biome introduces new obstacles, collectibles and enviromental challenges while retaining the same simple control scheme.
